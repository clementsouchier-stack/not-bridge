Submission terms TBA

Who can submit music?

Artists, labels, publishers and other rights holders, subject to the event’s eligibility rules.

How many tracks can I submit?

Maximum TBA tracks per participant. This limit is configurable by event.

Will every submitted track be played?

No. Music supervisors search the submitted catalog according to their briefs. Only tracks appearing in relevant searches may be listened to during the session.

Does being selected guarantee a sync?

No. The Open Session creates genuine discovery and professional exposure, but no synchronization can be guaranteed.
