SUNO · LAST REVIEWED AUG 2026

Commercial permission does not remove the underlying rights question.

Paid-plan terms provide commercial-use language, while copyright vesting is not warranted and provider indemnity is not offered to the user. Litigation remains part of the legal context.

ELEVENLABS MUSIC · LAST REVIEWED AUG 2026

Commercial use can still be media-specific.

Generated music may carry commercial-use rights on paid plans, while self-serve terms reviewed in August excluded film, TV and radio. Enterprise terms therefore matter for professional audiovisual use.

ADOBE FIREFLY · LAST REVIEWED AUG 2026

Indemnity can depend on the exact product surface and plan.

Adobe provides an indemnity route for eligible Firefly features on qualifying plans, subject to conditions. Production teams should verify the exact feature, plan and contract in force.

What triggers an update?

A new or revised provider term, a change in indemnity or transfer rights, a major licensing agreement, a material court development, a change in AI-detection/provenance policy, or another event that changes the professional risk assessment.

Editorial rule: if a point cannot be established from a sufficiently reliable source, we label it unclear rather than fill the gap.
