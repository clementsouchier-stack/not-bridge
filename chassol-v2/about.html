About

Christophe Chassol

Composer, pianist, arranger, filmmaker, musical director and presenter. His work moves between classical training, jazz, pop, cinema, contemporary art and the transformation of everyday sound into music.

Background

Piano from childhood, classical and jazz training, philosophy studies, contemporary-jazz ensembles, orchestration and a long period composing and arranging for image before his solo ultrascores came to the foreground.

Method

Chassol calls his audiovisual compositions ultrascores: sounds already present in an image are sampled, looped, transcribed and harmonized until image and music become one form.
