Method

Harmonizing reality

Speech, birds, movement, cities and images become musical material. Chassol samples the real, loops it, transcribes it and reharmonizes it into what he calls an ultrascore.

01

Speech

Voices become melody

A sentence already contains pitch, rhythm and phrasing. Chassol makes those contours audible.

02

Nature

Birdsong as score

From Messiaen to Ultrabirdz, natural sound is treated as composition rather than atmosphere.

03

Stand-up

Funny How?

The melodic life of spoken comedy, filmed and reworked between Chicago, New York and Los Angeles.

About

From arranging to ultrascore

Classically trained, jazz educated and shaped by cinema, Chassol moved through conducting, arranging, music for images and pop collaboration before turning harmonized reality into a body of work of his own.

1980s–90sTraining

Piano, classical studies, jazz and philosophy.

1990s–2000sConductor / arranger

Ensembles, orchestration, advertising, cinema and television.

2000sPhoenix / Tellier

Arranging, keyboards, touring and pop studio work.

2011–15Ultrascores

Nola Chérie, Indiamore and Big Sun.

2017–24Art / broadcast

Studio Venezia, France Musique, Ground Control, Musée d’Orsay and Basquiat.
